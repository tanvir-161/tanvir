About Me

I am Tanvir Ahmed, an Assistant Merchandiser based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I have nearly five years of professional experience in the apparel merchandising industry.

My expertise includes end-to-end order management, strategic sourcing, garment costing, critical path management, product development, sampling, production planning and shipment execution.

I have worked with international brands including NEXT UK PLC and Betty Barclay, coordinating with buyers, factories, mills, suppliers, quality teams and logistics partners to ensure smooth production and timely delivery.
